Getting into the brain

Once-daily dosing · 42 days

Aripiprazole is absorbed over 3–5 hours and leaves slowly (half-life about 75 hours). It builds up for roughly two weeks before levels stop rising, which is one reason benefits and some side effects take time to appear.

Aripiprazole is a partial agonist. When it sits on a D2 receptor it switches the receptor on, but only to roughly a quarter or a third of what dopamine does. So it lowers the signal where dopamine is high and can raise it where dopamine is low. How strong that partial signal feels depends on how many spare receptors a tissue has (receptor reserve). The pituitary has a lot, so there aripiprazole acts almost like dopamine itself.
