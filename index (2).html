Aripiprazole Brain Simulations

Two interactive simulations of how aripiprazole (Abilify) acts in the brain. Both run the same pharmacology; one follows a simulated patient who responds well, the other a simulated patient who develops side effects.

These pages are educational simulations built from published research. They are not a medical device and not medical advice, and the patients shown are simulated. Do not use them to make any decision about a medication. Full terms are at the bottom of each simulation.

When it works

A simulated patient on 10 mg a day with normal metabolism. Dopamine signaling settles and symptoms ease over a few weeks.
